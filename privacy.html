Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Barvassi ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website barvassi.com and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and password (encrypted).
  • Profile Information: Avatar images, Barvassi ID, verification status, subscription status.
  • Communications: Any messages or inquiries you send to us.

b. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interactions with our website.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use local storage to maintain your session securely. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Barvassi vault account.
  • To authenticate your identity and protect your data.
  • To process account upgrades and verification.
  • To communicate important updates (security, policy changes).
  • To improve our services and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with robust encryption protocols. We treat your personal information like a sealed vault – accessible only to you and our core systems.

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS.
  • Access to user data is strictly controlled.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with essential service providers:

  • Supabase – for authentication, database, and file storage (avatars).
  • Vercel – for website hosting and deployment.

These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and comply with privacy regulations.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@barvassi.com

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Barvassi is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through a notice on the platform.
