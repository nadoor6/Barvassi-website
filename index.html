About

Barvassi is an independent design practice founded on the belief that great design is invisible — it serves the message, not itself.

We work with ambitious founders, companies, luxury brands, governments, and institutions to build visual systems that endure. Our approach is strategic, editorial, and timeless. We don't decorate — we articulate.

Based in Aleppo. Working globally.
